Practice areas

Matters handled before the courts and tribunals in Kanpur and the surrounding region.

Criminal law

FIR and police matters, regular and anticipatory bail, cheque bounce, cheating and breach of trust.

Civil law

Civil suits, recovery of money, contract disputes, legal notices and consumer matters.

Family and matrimonial law

Divorce, maintenance, child custody, domestic violence and restitution of conjugal rights.

Property law

Sale deeds, partition, tenancy and eviction, wills, succession and title disputes.

Cyber law

Online fraud, identity misuse, defamation and other cyber offences.

Labour and service matters

Employment disputes, service law and labour court proceedings.

Motor accident claims

Claims before the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal.

Banking and tax

Loan recovery, SARFAESI matters, GST and income tax disputes.

How I work

Three kinds of service, depending on what your situation needs.

Consultation

A direct discussion of your facts and documents, followed by clear advice on your options.

Documentation

Drafting and review of notices, agreements, deeds and affidavits, and help with registration.

Litigation

Filing, defending and following up matters through every stage of a court case.
